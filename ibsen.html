Yadeni Eba presents plays by Ibsen

The Wild Duck

Act III

Hialmar

No, leave it alone. I can manage quite well without breakfast.

[Sitting down at the table.]

Where is Hedvig?

Gina

She is in the kitchen, helping with the coffee.

Hialmar

Ah, let her stay there.

Hedvig

[Enters bringing the coffee pot.]

Good morning, father! Here is your coffee.

Hialmar

I don't want any coffee. Take it away.

Hedvig

[Timidly.]

But father, it's fresh brewed for you.

Gina

Let your father be, Hedvig. He has a lot on his mind this morning.
